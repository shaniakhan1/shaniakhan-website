Shania Khan

Books, ideas, and daily practices for a life that feels like yours.

Shania Khan is the author of Rich Grandma Energy and The Goodbye Theory, and the founder of Soft60.

Her work connects identity, wealth, personal style, nervous system regulation, and soft power. Across her books, essays, and daily practices, she explores how to build a calmer, richer life with less chaos.

The work

From the idea
to everyday life.

Shania writes about standards, endings, self-trust, and the choices that shape a life. Rich Grandma Energy explores identity, money, and softness. The Goodbye Theory brings 19 perspectives to endings and the lives that become possible when something is allowed to be over.

She created Soft60 as a daily practice and community. The app includes daily invitations from Shania, journaling, rituals, breathwork, and reflection.

Vogue France has featured Shania’s perspective on clothing and identity, and she has appeared on Style Your Mind to discuss ambition, personal style, and her idea that chaos repels wealth.
