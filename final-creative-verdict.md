# Final Creative Verdict: ShaniaKhan.com

## Where the site is now

The site now feels like **one authored world** rather than a set of isolated landing pages. The clearest proof is in the route between Home, Shop, Soft60, Journal, Advisory, and Media Kit: each page now carries the same calm, confident premise while doing a different job.

The website has moved from a generic information-and-link collection into a credible brand experience. It gives a new visitor a reason to stay, a practical way to begin, and a clearer picture of who Shania is before asking them to buy.

## What now works together

| Area | Creative role | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | The front door | Founder-led four-chapter journey with clear paths into Shop, Soft60, Journal, and Advisory. |
| Shop | The sales room | Repaired product layouts, real planner previews, richer product language, paired buying paths, and calm opt-in ambient moments. |
| Soft60 | The core methodology | Light, clear, and protected; assessment, sign-in, shop, and support paths remain intact. |
| Journal | The trust engine | Each refreshed article now points readers to the current relevant practice, product, or Advisory route—not a stale funnel. |
| Advisory | The high-intent conversation | High-contrast ocean-backed page with direct, humane language and a preserved inquiry workflow. |
| Media Kit | The credibility layer | Correct `@iamshaniakhan` handle, 7M monthly views, and fully clickable social routes. |
| Support and Privacy | The utility layer | Clear and calm cool-neutral pages with current shared navigation and no unnecessary marketing friction. |

## The creative truth

> The site is now strong enough that a visitor can reasonably think: “This woman has a point of view, an actual body of work, and a place for me to start.”

It is not yet at its final ceiling because the next level requires **more original evidence**, not more CSS. The most powerful future additions are a true editorial product shoot, more real planner-page photography, occasional new founder film, verified press or speaking proof, and product pages that give every purchase its own sense of occasion.

## Deliberate decisions

The public Journal no longer routes visitors to the inactive Mastermind waitlist or the retired FrequencyPlanner.com storefront. Its updated calls to action now go to current Shop, Soft60, Advisory, and Substack paths. The legacy `/mastermind/` and protected `/the-room/` routes were not deleted or altered; they remain unpromoted until an intentional offer decision is made.

Ambient ocean and Tuscany films are intentionally sparse and muted by default. Sound only plays after a visitor chooses it. This protects accessibility, attention, and page speed while still giving the site a sensory signature.

## Next stage when ready

The next strategic project is a **product-evidence campaign**, not another site-wide rebuild. Shoot or collect 5–10 real product detail images for each planner family, then create individual product moments with delivery detail, contents, print-versus-digital choice, and use-case storytelling. That is the work that will make the collection not only beautiful, but indisputably easy to buy from.
