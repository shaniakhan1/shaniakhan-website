# Full-Site Creative Direction

## The experience

The site will feel like an unhurried editorial retreat, not an application, a template, or a product catalogue. The point is not constant animation. The point is rhythm: still product art, breathing room, a few cinematic pauses, and clear opportunities to buy when a visitor is ready.

## Motion system

| Moment | Placement | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Ocean at first light | Homepage and the Soft60 transition | Muted by default, short looping cinematic background, static image fallback |
| Tuscany in a light breeze | Between ritual products and planners | Muted by default, short looping cinematic background, static image fallback |
| Warm interior light | Journal and consultation transition | Muted by default, short looping cinematic background, static image fallback |
| Sound | Optional only | No autoplay sound. A visitor-controlled sound button can be offered where audio supports the clip. |

## Commerce content architecture

Every product will answer three questions without a visitor needing to leave the page: **what it is, what is inside, and why it belongs in her life now.** The shop will use recovered Shopify language selectively, not dump raw product-page copy into a grid. Each planner will pair an authentic cover or flat-lay with an expandable preview and a concise contents list.

## Visual rules

The system remains light, tactile, and calm: ivory, warm stone, natural black, muted olive, warm clay, and sunlit gold. Buttons use dark text on light surfaces or light text on reliably dark surfaces. No neon, no unexplained dark panels, and no section that exists solely to fill space.
