# Rich Grandma Energy: The Book, the Concept, the Movement

**Published by Shania Khan**

Rich Grandma Energy started as a feeling I could not name any other way.

You know the woman. She walks into a room and something shifts. She is not performing. She is not trying to impress anyone. She is not hustling for her position in the conversation. She is simply there, fully, without apology. She has already decided she is worth the room. She does not need you to confirm it.

That is Rich Grandma Energy. And I wrote a book about it.

## What the Book Is About

Rich Grandma Energy: The Death of the Good Girl is a No. 1 Amazon bestselling book about the internal shift that separates women who build things from women who keep getting ready to build things. It is about the death of the performance. The end of the apology. The beginning of operating from actual power rather than the appearance of it.

The book is not a self-help book in the traditional sense. It is not a list of habits or a morning routine or a five-step framework. It is a permission slip. A direct conversation about what it actually takes to stop shrinking and start moving from a completely different internal state.

You can find it on Amazon at [Rich Grandma Energy](https://www.amazon.com/Rich-Grandma-Energy-Death-Girl/dp/B0GKP22M1R/).

## The Concept

Rich Grandma Energy is the energy of a woman who has already done the work. Who has already decided. Who is not waiting for external validation to move. She is not performing youth, or hustle, or accessibility. She is operating from a place of settled, grounded, unshakeable clarity.

The "grandma" part is intentional. It is about the woman who has lived enough to stop caring about the wrong things. Who has earned her perspective. Who moves through the world with the quiet authority of someone who knows exactly who she is.

The "rich" part is not just about money, though it includes money. It is about richness of experience, of presence, of internal resource. A woman with Rich Grandma Energy is rich in the way that actually matters: she is full. She is not operating from lack.

## Why It Became a Movement

The book resonated because it named something women had been feeling but could not articulate. The exhaustion of performing. The cost of the good girl. The quiet grief of having spent years being palatable when what you actually wanted was to be powerful.

Women started sharing it with each other. They started using the phrase in their own lives, their own businesses, their own conversations. It became shorthand for a specific kind of shift: from performing to operating. From fear to power. From hustle to soft power.

## The Connection to Soft Power

Rich Grandma Energy and soft power are two sides of the same thing. Rich Grandma Energy is the identity. Soft power is the operating system. The woman with Rich Grandma Energy moves through the world using soft power: she does not force, she does not perform, she does not fight for her position. She simply holds it.

The work I do, through the Soft60 app, the Frequency Planner, and the Soft Power Mastermind, is all in service of helping women access this. Not as a concept. As a lived experience. As the state they operate from every day.
