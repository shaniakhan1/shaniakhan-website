What Is the Soft60 App and How Does It Work

By Shania Khan

I built the Soft60 app because I could not find anything that did what I needed it to do. Everything out there was either a habit tracker dressed up in wellness language, or a meditation app that felt like it was designed for someone who had already figured everything out. Neither of those was useful to me.

Soft60 is different. It is a 60-day nervous system reset built for women who are high-functioning on the outside and quietly exhausted on the inside. Women who have the strategy, the vision, the work ethic, and still find themselves stuck in the same patterns. Not because they are not trying hard enough. Because they are operating from the wrong internal state.

What the App Actually Does

The Soft60 app guides you through a 60-day protocol that works at the physiological level, not just the cognitive one. Most wellness tools ask you to think differently. Soft60 asks your body to feel differently first. The thinking follows.

Inside the app you will find daily calibrations, which are short, specific practices designed to shift your nervous system state before you move into your day. These are not affirmations. They are not journaling prompts. They are somatic and energetic practices that take between five and fifteen minutes and are designed to be done consistently.

The app also includes audio drops, a private community, and a progress tracker that shows you where you are in the 60-day arc. Every feature is designed to keep you in the work, not just thinking about the work.

Why 60 Days

Sixty days is not arbitrary. It is the minimum amount of time required to begin rewiring a nervous system pattern that has been running for years. Most programs ask you to change your behaviour in a weekend. That is not how the body works.

The research on neuroplasticity is clear: consistent, repeated practice over time is what creates new neural pathways. Sixty days of daily practice is enough to feel a measurable shift. It is not the end of the work. It is the beginning of a completely different relationship with yourself.

Who Soft60 Is For

Soft60 is for women who are done performing calm and ready to actually feel it. Women who are building businesses, raising families, navigating transitions, and doing all of it from a place of low-grade stress that has become so familiar they have stopped noticing it.

It is for the woman who has tried every productivity system and still feels behind. The woman who knows what she wants and cannot seem to make herself move toward it. The woman who is tired of being told to think positive when what she actually needs is to regulate her nervous system.

How to Get Started

You can find the Soft60 app at soft60.app. The onboarding takes about five minutes and walks you through the protocol before you begin. Day one is available immediately.

The 60-day program is self-paced, which means you move through it on your own schedule. Most women do one calibration per day, in the morning before they open their phone or start their work. That is the recommendation. But the app is designed to meet you where you are.

If you want a guided place to begin, take the Soft60 assessment. It will give you a clear starting point inside the existing experience.

Begin with the practice, not another promise. The Soft60 assessment will help you choose a starting point inside the existing 60-day experience.
