# ShaniaKhan.com: Candid Conversion Review

**Review date:** August 19, 2026  
**Scope:** The public home, shop, Soft60, Journal, Advisory, Media Kit, support, and privacy paths after the redesign.

## The honest answer

>The site now has a real point of view. I would understand why Shania Khan is interesting, what her work is about, and where to begin. The homepage, shop, Soft60 page, and rebuilt Advisory page now feel like they belong to the same woman.

>Would I purchase today? **Yes, if I already feel aligned with the voice and enter through Soft60, a book, or a specific planner.** The site is no longer merely a directory of checkout links. It has atmosphere, a clear founder perspective, and credible entry points.

>Would I call it complete at the standard of a deeply considered premium brand? **Not yet.** The next gains will come from stronger individual-product evidence and a final consistency pass across the pages that still use the older template language.

## The highest-value next improvements

| Priority | Improvement | Why it matters | Recommended approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Build richer individual product moments | A buyer needs to know what is inside a $22–$44 digital product before checking out. | Add one expandable “what you receive” block, 3–5 real preview pages, format and delivery details, and a clear use case to each featured item. |
| **2** | Clarify digital versus print choices | Some collection items exist in multiple forms; buyers should never need to infer which one they are purchasing. | Pair digital and print options on a shared product moment with visible format, price, and fulfillment labels. |
| **3** | Add founder-led proof without inventing testimonials | The brand needs more evidence of Shania’s expertise, but only authentic evidence. | Use real press, podcast appearances, speaking clips, published-book pages, follower metrics, and original media—not synthetic reviews. |
| **4** | Standardize the older page templates | Legacy support and privacy should stay simple, but their navigation and footer should match the new system. | Give low-intent pages the shared navigation, type scale, footer, and concise utility-first structure. |
| **5** | Convert the Journal into a stronger acquisition loop | The Journal has brand value but needs better pathways into the collection. | Give every article one related product, one Soft60 path, and one Substack invitation that fits the article topic. |

## Recommended order of operations

The next highest-return work is not more ambient video. It is **product evidence**: real preview spreads, clear contents, delivery explanation, and digital-versus-print choices. After that, move through the Journal article templates and the remaining utility pages for visual consistency. This sequence gives a prospective buyer more reason to trust the collection before adding more atmosphere.
