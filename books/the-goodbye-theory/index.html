About the book

For the endings that make room for a different life.

The Goodbye Theory explores the space between something ending and allowing yourself to acknowledge it. Across 19 theories, Shania Khan writes about relationships, identities, timing, and the versions of ourselves we keep carrying after we have outgrown them.
