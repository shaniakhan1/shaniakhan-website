About the book

Standards. Identity. Money. A life that feels like yours.

Rich Grandma Energy brings Shania Khan’s work on soft power into a book about the way a woman moves through her life. It explores what changes when external approval stops setting the terms, and when ambition has room for softness, taste, and self-trust.
