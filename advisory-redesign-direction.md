# Advisory Page Direction

The page will open with a quiet ocean visual, but the information panel stays solid and high contrast. The visual provides calm; it does not carry essential copy.

The tone changes from gatekeeping to direct support. The lead message becomes: **“You do not have to have the perfect words. Tell me what is happening, where you feel stuck, and what you want to be different.”** The page will describe the session as a grounded outside perspective for a business decision, brand shift, next chapter, or season that needs clearer direction.

The form will retain the existing inquiry delivery route while replacing intimidating revenue, waitlist, and “mastery” language with simpler questions about the support the visitor needs. The form will retain required choices so Shania receives usable context, but no question will imply that help is reserved for a particular income level or certainty level.
