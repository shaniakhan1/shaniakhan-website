# Shania Khan Storefront Direction

## Visual premise

The storefront will feel like a digital magazine that happens to sell a collection: cinematic, graphic, and confident rather than muted, precious, or template-driven. Product covers are the visual evidence; the page should frame them with strong color, scale, whitespace, and editorial pacing.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight ink | `#151329` | Primary field, navigation, and high-contrast type |
| Ultraviolet | `#5A33C6` | Hero and featured collection field |
| Acid lime | `#C8FF3D` | Buying cues, labels, and primary action emphasis |
| Punch coral | `#FF6B60` | Secondary emphasis and category contrast |
| Electric blue | `#79A8FF` | Digital-planner category and supporting detail |
| Porcelain | `#F8F7F2` | Breathing room and readable content surfaces |

The palette deliberately avoids the prior cream, tan, and brown treatment. Product cover colors carry the nuance; the system provides a graphic, editorial frame.

## Purchase journey

`ShaniaKhan.com` will carry the customer journey. The homepage navigation and primary calls to action will point to `/shop/`. The shop will orient visitors with an entry hero, then let them discover the collection by intention: start with Soft60, choose a digital ritual tool, shop a digital planner, or select a printed planner. Product cards will show the cover, outcome, price, format, and a clear direct-buy action.
