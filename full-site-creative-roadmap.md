# Full-Site Creative Roadmap

## The strategic verdict

The redesigned Home, Shop, Soft60, Advisory, Journal, and Media Kit now form the brand’s strongest public spine. The high-return work left is not a broad aesthetic reset. It is the quieter work that turns attention into trust: stronger product evidence, article-level paths to action, and a consistent utility shell.

## Priority 1: Product evidence

The collection needs more confidence-building detail before each external checkout. The site already has real covers and a planner gallery; the next pass should make each category answer four questions plainly: what arrives, what is inside, who it is for, and how to use it. Digital and print variants should appear beside one another wherever both exist.

## Priority 2: Journal-to-practice pathways

Each Journal article should end with a compact, topic-aware module that connects the reader to one relevant product, the Soft60 assessment, and Substack. This is not a generic sales banner. A money article should lead to the Wealth planner; an ending/identity article should lead to The Goodbye Theory; a nervous-system article should lead to Soft60.

## Priority 3: Utility consistency

Legacy Support and Privacy should be practical and quick, not immersive editorial experiences. They need the current shared navigation, a readable cool-neutral surface, and a simple footer; their legal and support content should remain unchanged.

## Priority 4: Mastermind decision

The legacy public Mastermind waitlist conflicts with the current approved public funnel. It should remain unpromoted until the owner chooses one of three intentional states: archive it, redirect it to Private Advisory, or relaunch it with a verified offer, fulfillment plan, and dedicated product experience.

## Priority 5: Campaign cadence

The ambient films work because they are sparse. Future campaigns should add one new founder portrait or original short film at a time, then allow the rest of the site to breathe. The site will become more powerful through editorial curation, not continuous animation.
