## HOT SEAT GUIDE

The hot seat is where the transformation happens. The slides are context. The hot seat is the work.

**How to open a hot seat:**
"Would you be open to being in the hot seat for a few minutes?"

**How to start:**
"Tell me what is actually going on." Then stop talking. Let her speak. Do not interrupt. Listen for what is underneath the words.

**What to listen for:**
- The file that is running (Worth, Safety, or Enough)
- The decision she is avoiding
- The thing she is softening
- The room she is staying in past its time

**How to close:**
"What do you know now that you did not know 10 minutes ago?"

**If she cries:**
Do not rush. Do not say "it is okay." Say: "Stay with that. That is important." Give it 30 seconds. Then continue.

**Time:**
10 minutes per person. Set a soft timer. It is okay to go to 12–13 if something important is happening.

## WHAT TO DO IF THINGS GO WRONG

**Someone does not show up:**
DM them after the call: "We missed you today. The recording is in The Room. How are you doing?"

**Technical issues (Zoom crashes, audio fails):**
Stay calm. Post in The Room community: "Having a tech issue — back in 5 minutes." Rejoin. Continue.

**Someone is disruptive or negative:**
Address it privately in a DM after the call. Never call someone out in front of the group.

**Fewer than 20 women join:**
Run it exactly the same. A smaller room is often more powerful. Do not mention the number.

**Someone asks for a refund:**
Have a clear policy before the cohort starts. Recommended: no refunds after June 28 (48 hours before first call). All sales are final after that date.
