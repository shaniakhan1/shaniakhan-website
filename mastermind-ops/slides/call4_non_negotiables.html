Your three
non-
negotiables.
These are the three things you will not go back on.

Not a goal. Not an intention. A line.

Write them now, while you are still in this room.
Non-Negotiable One
My price floor is _______.
The number you will not go below. Not the number that feels safe. The number that is true.
Non-Negotiable Two
The content I will no longer soften:
The opinion. The story. The truth you have been adding disclaimers to. Name it.
Non-Negotiable Three
The room I am leaving:
The relationship, the platform, the dynamic, the version of yourself. Name the room. Then leave it.
Write them. Then live them.
