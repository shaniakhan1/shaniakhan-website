Call Two · The Internal Update
What the File Says
01
"Money is hard."
You watched the adults in your life struggle with it. You learned that money requires suffering to obtain and causes suffering when it leaves.
02
"Money changes people."
You saw wealth make someone cold, selfish, or disconnected. You decided, somewhere deep down, that staying broke was staying safe — and staying good.
03
"I am not the kind of person who has money."
People like you do not end up wealthy. That is for other people. Different families. Different backgrounds. Not you.
04
"I have to earn my place."
You over-deliver, over-explain, and over-qualify because you are still trying to prove you deserve to be in the room. You have been proving it your whole life.
Which one is yours?
