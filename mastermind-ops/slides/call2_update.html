Call Two · The Internal Update
You Can Update the File
The old file
I have to earn
my place.
Discount before being asked
Over-deliver to justify the price
Wait until you feel ready to raise rates
Apologize for your prices
Stay in rooms that stopped serving you
The updated file
My place is not
earned. It is claimed.
State the price. No qualifier after it.
Deliver what you promised. Nothing more.
Raise the rate. Let the discomfort be information.
Say the price the way you say your name.
Leave when it is time. Without explanation.
The file does not update through thinking. It updates through acting from a different place and letting the evidence accumulate.
