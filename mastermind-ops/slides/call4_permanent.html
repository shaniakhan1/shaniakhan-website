The Integration Protocol
5
min daily
The Regulation Check
Before any important decision, piece of content, or conversation. Three breaths. One question: "Am I operating from fear or from worth?" That is the whole practice.
15
min weekly
The File Audit
Review one decision you made this week. Was it from the old file or the updated one? What would the updated file have done differently? Write it down. Do not judge it. Just notice.
1x
quarterly
The Rooms Audit
Which rooms are still yours? Which ones have you been staying in past their time? Which new rooms are you ready to enter? Rich people leave the party first. Do the audit.
