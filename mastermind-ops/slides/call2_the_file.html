Call 2 · The Internal Update
You have a file on money.
It was written before you were ten.
Where it was written
Before you knew
what money was.
—
Watching your parents fight about bills
—
Hearing "we cannot afford that"
—
Seeing what happened to people who had it
—
Learning that wanting more was dangerous
Where it is running right now
In every decision
you make today.
—
The price you quoted last week
—
The discount you gave without being asked
—
The rate you have not raised yet
—
The ask you have been putting off
