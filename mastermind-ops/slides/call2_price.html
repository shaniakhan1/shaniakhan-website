Call Two · The Internal Update
Your price is
not about your
worth.
It is about what you believe about your worth.

Those are different things.
One of them is changeable.
When you undercharge
"I am not sure I am worth this."
The discount you gave without being asked. The rate you have not raised in two years. The proposal you sent and then immediately regretted.
When you charge your real number
"I know what this is worth."
Not arrogance. Clarity. The price is information about where you are operating from. It tells the client what to expect before you say a word.
What changes the price
"The file changes. The number follows."
You do not raise your rates by deciding to raise your rates. You raise them by updating the story running underneath them.
The number you are afraid to say out loud is probably the right one.
