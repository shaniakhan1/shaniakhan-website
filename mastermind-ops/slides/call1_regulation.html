The Reframe
What people think it means
Being calm.
Feeling nothing.
What it actually means
Not being
run by
what you feel.
Reacting
Your nervous system is in charge. The feeling arrives and you act from it immediately. You undercharge because the number felt scary. You soften the post because the comment section felt dangerous.
Responding
You feel the feeling and you choose what to do with it. The fear is still there. The price goes up anyway. The post goes out anyway. The room gets left anyway.
"Regulation is not the absence of feeling.
It is the presence of choice."
