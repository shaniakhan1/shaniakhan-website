What Soft Power Actually Is
What people think it means
Being nice.
Being quiet.
Being agreeable.
What it actually means
Operating from
choice,
not fear.
The three markers
You respond. You do not react.
Your actions come from a regulated state, not from whatever feeling just arrived. You choose your next move.
You say the true thing.
Not the softened version. Not the version with the disclaimer. The thing you actually believe, said clearly.
You leave when it is time.
Rooms, relationships, rates, roles. You do not stay past the expiration date because leaving feels dangerous.
