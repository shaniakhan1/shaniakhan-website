Call Two · The Internal Update
How the File Shows Up
You will recognize yourself in at least one of these. Probably more than one.
01
Undercharging
You quote a number and immediately feel like it is too much. So you lower it before they even respond.
02
Over-delivering
You give three times what you promised because you are still trying to prove you were worth the price.
03
Apologizing for prices
"I know it is a lot." "I can work with your budget." You said it before they said anything.
04
Waiting to raise rates
When you have more testimonials. When the economy is better. When you feel more ready. The file keeps moving the goalposts.
05
Uninvited discounts
They did not ask. You offered anyway. Because some part of you was not sure they would say yes at full price.
None of this is a strategy problem. It is a file problem.
