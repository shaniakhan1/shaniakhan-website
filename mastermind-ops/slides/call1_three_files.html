The Operating System
The Three Files Running Your Life
Every decision you make runs through one of these. Most women do not know they exist.
01
The Worth File
What you believe you deserve. Drives every pricing decision, negotiation, and ask you make.
Charging less than you know you should
Giving discounts nobody asked for
Waiting until you feel "ready enough"
02
The Safety File
What your nervous system believes is safe to be. Visible. Loud. Wealthy. Different.
Softening your most powerful opinions
Staying small to avoid the comment section
Performing instead of being seen
03
The Enough File
What you believe about rest and existing without producing. The robe theory lives here.
Rest that feels like guilt
Always being "on" and earning your place
Never owning a robe
