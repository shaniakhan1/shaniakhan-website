Call 3 · Visibility Without Performance
Your body is not
broken.
It is outdated.
The nervous system that kept you safe at seven years old is still running the same threat detection software.

It does not know the difference between a predator and a comment section.

Regulation is not a wellness practice. It is what makes visibility possible.
What the body reads as danger
The trigger
Posting a controversial opinion
What the nervous system hears
"You are about to be rejected by the group." Historically, rejection meant death. The body does not know you are just posting a Reel.
The trigger
Stating a high price out loud
What the nervous system hears
"You are claiming more than you deserve." The body braces for punishment. So you lower the number before they can punish you.
The trigger
Being seen without performing
What the nervous system hears
"If they see the real you, they will leave." So you polish. You qualify. You add the disclaimer. You perform safety before they can take it from you.
Regulation is not the goal. It is the prerequisite.
