# DECK 2: The Internal Update
**Call 2 — July 7, 12:00 PM CDT**

## Cover
**The Internal Update**
*Soft Power Mastermind · Week 2*
Shania Khan

## Slide 1
**Last week you identified the files.**
This week we start rewriting them.

## Slide 2
**The file is not the truth.**
It is a survival strategy that worked once.
It kept you safe when you were small.
It is costing you everything now.

## Slide 3
**The Money Wound**
Most women in this room did not grow up watching money be easy.
They watched it be a source of stress, silence, shame, or scarcity.
That is the file running your pricing.
Not your skill level. Not your market. The file.

## Slide 4
**What the file sounds like**
- "I do not want to seem greedy"
- "They might not be able to afford it"
- "I should offer a discount"
- "Who am I to charge that?"
- "I need to prove myself first"

*Every one of these is a survival response, not a business decision.*

## Slide 5
**The price is not the problem.**
Your relationship to being seen is the problem.
Charging more means being more visible.
Your nervous system is protecting you from that.

## Slide 6
**The Update**
You are not seven years old anymore.
You are not dependent on the approval of the people in that original room.
You have evidence — real evidence — that you are worth more than you are charging.
The file has not caught up yet.

## Slide 7
**How to update the file**
1. Name the old belief out loud (writing counts)
2. Find the evidence that contradicts it
3. Make one decision from the new belief before your nervous system talks you out of it
4. Repeat until the new belief is the default

## Slide 8
**The one-decision rule**
You do not have to believe it fully yet.
You just have to make one decision from the new belief.
Raise the price. Send the email. Post the thing.
The belief follows the action. Not the other way around.

## Slide 9
**This week's work**
Use the Nervous System Reset before any pricing conversation, important email, or piece of content that feels risky.
Notice which file activates. Name it.
Make one decision from the updated file.

## Slide 10
**Hot Seat**
*Who has been sitting with something since last week?*

## Closing Slide
**The file is not you.**
It was written for a version of you that no longer exists.

# DECK 3: Visibility Without Performance
**Call 3 — July 14, 12:00 PM CDT**

## Cover
**Visibility Without Performance**
*Soft Power Mastermind · Week 3*
Shania Khan

## Slide 1
**There are two kinds of visibility.**
Performed visibility: showing up to be approved of.
Soft power visibility: showing up because you have something true to say.
One exhausts you. One builds you.

## Slide 2
**You know the difference.**
Performed content feels like a costume.
You post it and immediately want to delete it.
You check the numbers obsessively.
You feel hollow even when it performs well.

## Slide 3
**Soft power content feels like relief.**
You post it and feel lighter.
You do not need to check the numbers because you already know it was true.
The right people find it. The wrong people leave. Both are correct.

## Slide 4
**Why women perform instead of appear**
- Performing feels safer than being seen
- If the performance fails, you can say it was not really you
- Real visibility means real rejection is possible
- And real rejection feels like proof the file was right

## Slide 5
**The most controversial thing you believe**
is your most valuable piece of content.
Not because controversy drives engagement.
Because truth drives trust.
And trust is what sells.

## Slide 6
**The Robe Theory. The Goodbye Theory.**
Both went viral not because they were provocative.
Because they were true.
And the people who needed to hear them had never heard anyone say it out loud.
That is your job.

## Slide 7
**Soft power content formula**
1. The observation (what you notice that others do not say)
2. The reframe (why it means something different than people think)
3. The permission (what the reader is now allowed to do)

## Slide 8
**You do not need a large audience.**
You need the right audience.
20 women in a room who trust you completely is worth more than 200,000 followers who do not.
Build for depth, not width.

## Slide 9
**This week's work**
Complete The Expansion Map in The Room.
Identify one piece of content you have been avoiding.
Post it before the next call.

## Slide 10
**Hot Seat**
*Who has been softening something?*

## Closing Slide
**The women who need you**
cannot find you when you are performing.
They can only find you when you are real.
*See you July 21.*
