## UNIVERSAL CALL RULES

**Hot seat format:**
- Ask permission first: "Would you be open to being in the hot seat for a few minutes?"
- Start with: "Tell me what is actually going on."
- Do not coach from the head. Coach from what you hear underneath the words.
- End every hot seat with: "What do you know now that you did not know 10 minutes ago?"

**If someone cries:**
Do not rush past it. Do not say "it is okay." Say: "Stay with that. That is important." Give it 30 seconds. Then continue.

**If the call runs long:**
Cut the teaching, not the hot seat. The teaching is in the slides. The transformation is in the hot seat.

**If someone is quiet:**
At the end of the call, DM them privately: "I noticed you were quiet today. How are you doing?"
