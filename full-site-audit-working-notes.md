# Full-Site Creative Audit: Working Notes

## High-confidence findings from visual review

| Page | Strength | Gap | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/the-room/` | Clear, focused password gate for members. | The old dark visual system and “Soft Power Mastermind” language are deliberately separate from the current public brand. It should not be promoted from current navigation. | Keep as a protected legacy member area unless the owner decides to relaunch it with a new offer. |
| `/legacy-support/` | Clear utility flow, concise explanations, and safe email routes for historical purchases. | It still uses the older cream-and-gold system and repeats a more editorial visual cadence than a support page needs. | Keep utility-first; later apply the current shared header/footer and cool neutral accessibility tokens without adding more marketing content. |

The priority remains product evidence and consistent public-page pathways. Protected legacy and utility pages should be clarified, not overdesigned.

| Page | Strength | Gap | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/blog/` | Strong editorial opening and a good calm pause; articles are easy to scan. | It includes a Mastermind article and does not yet systematically connect each editorial topic to a relevant product or Soft60 next step. | Keep its layout. Add an article-template “continue your practice” module with one relevant product, Soft60, and Substack path. |
| `/mastermind/` | The waitlist is visually clean and technically simple. | It is a publicly indexed offer and a waitlist funnel, while the current approved storefront strategy does not promote an active Mastermind. It also repeats the older cream-and-gold language. | Do not promote it. Recommend owner confirmation before either archiving it, redirecting it to Advisory, or formally reactivating it with a verified offer and fulfillment plan. |
| `/privacy/` | Clear explanation of information rights, a dedicated request path, and appropriate warning not to send sensitive payment data. | The dense navigation and older cream-and-gold design make a utility page feel more ornamental than necessary. | Preserve the legal content and request path; later apply the shared utility header/footer and simplified cool-neutral tokens. |
