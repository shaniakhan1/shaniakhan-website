# Homepage Redesign Direction

## The four chapters

| Chapter | Purpose | Primary destination |
|---|---|---|
| **The Point of View** | Introduce Shania as author, founder, and the voice behind the work through the existing founder image and a short, clear thesis. | Shop and media kit |
| **A Gentler Way** | Create one quiet ocean transition that makes the sensory language of Soft60 tangible, not decorative. | Soft60 assessment |
| **Choose Your Door** | Give visitors three legible entry paths: build a daily practice, shop the tools, or read the ideas. | Soft60, shop, journal/Substack |
| **The Collection and the Living World** | Make the products visual and concrete, then direct visitors to books, Substack, advisory, and media. | Shop, books, Substack, advisory, media kit |

## Copy standard

Every line must say something concrete. We will avoid abstract phrasing such as “another way to move through a life.” The ambient shop headline becomes: **“There is a gentler way to move through your day.”**

## Motion standard

The ocean footage appears once as a full-width, muted-by-default scene. It can be unmuted only through an explicit control. Cards and editorial panels use small hover lifts; there are no looping decorative animations or automatic sound.
