About Shania Khan

Founder brain. Editorial taste. A point of view people remember.

Shania Khan is a Houston-based author, founder, creator and brand strategist. She is the founder of Soft60 and FLP Marketing Group and the author of Rich Grandma Energy and The Goodbye Theory.

Her career spans two decades across luxury, beauty, marketing and regulated industries. Her audience has grown around a body of work connecting nervous system regulation with money, identity, beauty, ambition and the standards women build their lives around.

She built Soft60 as a non-technical founder, runs an agency, writes books, creates media and continues to develop original frameworks that move between culture, business and personal transformation.
