About Shania Khan

A beautiful life should not require a fried nervous system.

Shania Khan is an American author, founder and creator based in Houston, Texas. She is the founder of Soft60 and FLP Marketing Group and the author of Rich Grandma Energy and The Goodbye Theory.

Her work explores nervous system regulation, soft power, wealth identity, luxury and intentional living, including the belief that chaos repels wealth and that luxury can be a form of nervous system regulation.

She has spent two decades working across luxury, beauty, marketing and regulated industries while building an audience around a quieter way to create wealth, identity and a beautiful life.
