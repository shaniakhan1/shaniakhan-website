About Shania Khan

A beautiful life should not require a fried nervous system.

Shania Khan is an American author, founder and creator based in Houston, Texas. She is the founder of Soft60 and FLP Marketing Group and the author of Rich Grandma Energy and The Goodbye Theory.

Her work explores nervous system regulation, soft power, wealth identity, intentional luxury and the idea that money, beauty and safety are all frequencies your body learns to recognize.

She has spent two decades across luxury, beauty, marketing and regulated industries while building an audience around a quieter way to create wealth, identity and a beautiful life.
