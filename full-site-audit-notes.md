# Full-Site Visual Audit Notes

## Homepage

The homepage now provides the strongest existing foundation for the full-site system: a warm ivory field, editorial type, founder-led hero photography, a clean Shop navigation entry, and a collection section that connects brand narrative to commerce. The full-site edit should preserve this level of restraint rather than overwrite it.

## Shop

The repaired Soft60 card now has a balanced cover and copy column, visible high-contrast purchase controls, restored original-product detail, authentic Shopify preview imagery, and an optional muted-by-default ocean moment. The earlier excessive blank panel was traced to invalid nested anchors, not to the desired light visual direction.

## Remaining full-site priority

The Soft60, journal, consultation, media kit, legacy support, privacy, mastermind, and The Room templates need shared navigation, footer, visual rhythm, and restrained motion alignment. Their purpose-specific content must remain clear: conversion pages should not inherit noisy decorative treatments, and legal/support pages should stay calm and task-focused.
